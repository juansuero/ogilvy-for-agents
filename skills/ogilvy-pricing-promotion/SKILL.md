## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Available prices and contribution margins; Buyer perceptions and competitive context; Promotion terms and target segment; Conversion and retention data if known.

1. Define the commercial goal and relevant segment without assuming lower price is better.
2. Compare price as an outlay and possible quality signal; distinguish real quality from perceived desirability.
3. Consider payment terms, sampling, premiums and bundles as separate testable variables.
4. Model scenarios only from supported numbers; consider margin and downstream retention.
5. Review impact on brand positioning, expectations and repeat purchase; reject false scarcity and unsupported superiority.
6. Recommend a limited test, explicit stopping conditions and a documented decision criterion.

Provide the requested result at the appropriate depth. Possible outputs: Options and tradeoff comparison; Price/promotion test design; Economics, positioning and unresolved inputs.
