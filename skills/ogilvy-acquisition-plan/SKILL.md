## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Offer/audience; Business objective; Evidence and existing assets; Constraints and known resources.

1. Choose a clear primary purpose before selecting content or channel.
2. Explain how the communication helps the audience consider the offer.
3. Plan a visible and credible next step with low unnecessary effort.
4. Use channels only with supplied or researched rationale, not historic television rankings.
5. Define how inquiry quality, follow-up and commercial outcomes will be tracked.
6. Recommend a feasible plan and what remains to validate.
