## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Known buying participants; Offer facts; Decision/concerns of each role; Existing proposal or messages.

1. Identify actual roles and allow one person to hold several.
2. Map what each needs to understand and approve.
3. Keep a shared verified fact base.
4. Draft distinct emphasis: use details and exceptions for evaluation, business effect/cost for approval where applicable.
5. Avoid patronizing nontechnical recipients or withholding relevant risks.
6. Choose one tailored artifact or separate variants only when useful.
