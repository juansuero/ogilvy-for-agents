## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Communication goal; Audience and relevant insights; Positioning/brand context; Verified product facts; Channel constraints.

1. Use a concise brief and relevant facts rather than inventing insights.
2. Generate genuinely distinct conceptual routes only as many as needed.
3. Explain product connection, intended audience interpretation and brand association.
4. Review surprise, strategic fit, distinctiveness and potential continuity as heuristics.
5. Keep compelling ideas that do not rely on functional detail alone; check for irrelevant spectacle.
6. Develop a preferred route into example executions and identify what to test.
