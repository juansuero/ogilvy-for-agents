## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Offer facts and limits; Buyer group or alternatives; Candidate messages; Evidence supporting differences.

1. Identify what the offer does and whom it is for.
2. Separate positioning, brand personality and campaign execution.
3. Test whether each proposed benefit matters to that buyer.
4. Compare plausible routes: meaningful technical difference, quality/service reputation, low cost, or positively good.
5. State the proof needed and avoid compulsory numerical claims.
6. Recommend a route with explicit tradeoffs and unresolved assumptions.

### Additional guidance for chapters 12–20

- Check a new offer for buyer-perceptible difference alongside a familiar frame of reference.
