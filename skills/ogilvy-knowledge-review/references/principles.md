# Selected source principles

Paraphrases of selected material from *Ogilvy on Advertising*. Historical studies and assertions are not independently verified. Adaptations and limits belong to this toolkit.

The book was published in 1983; digital workflows, statistical techniques, and modern industry practices here are explicit modern adaptations.

## OG-021 · Chapter 3 · RESEARCHERS

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: Research should be intellectually honest, timely, understandable and useful rather than paralyzed by perfectionism.
Adaptation: Distinguish evidence, unknowns and limited conclusions; deliver a useful bounded analysis.
Limit: Urgency does not excuse manufactured certainty or cherry-picking.

## OG-022 · Chapter 3 · RESEARCHERS

Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: Failure to retrieve old research makes agencies repeat work after people leave.
Adaptation: Record the question, context, methods, results, limits and decision in a retrievable entry.
Limit: Tags and repository formats are implementation choices, not book prescriptions.

## OG-027 · Chapter 4 · DISCIPLINE WORKS / WHAT TO DO WITH YOUR PROFITS

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: The author recommends written goals checked against accomplishment and a reserve for difficult periods, while warning about ventures outside competence.
Adaptation: Evaluate workload against outcomes and consider resilience when judging commitments.

## OG-055 · Chapter 12 · Direct mail

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: Direct-response channels permit tracing responses and sales, supporting controlled improvements.
Adaptation: Distinguish measured action, acquisition, profit and long-term value; choose channel-specific measures.
Limit: Attribution can be incomplete in cross-channel journeys; do not say every test isolates a single cause.

## OG-057 · Chapter 12 · Direct mail

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: Use a profitable mailing as a control and test challengers against it, considering incremental cost and profit.
Adaptation: Keep the current winner in the comparison, define replacement thresholds and account for economics.
Limit: Do not discard proven control due to novelty; confirm that control remains effective today.

## OG-066 · Chapter 14 · Competing with Procter & Gamble

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: A successful strategy can persist while execution variations continue to be tested.
Adaptation: Separate strategic changes from execution tests; maintain a measured baseline.
Limit: Historical spending, daypart and TV practices are not modern universal benchmarks.

## OG-070 · Chapter 15 · Size of sample

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: For time-trend surveys, changes in sample composition or question wording can mimic a trend.
Adaptation: Compare equivalent cohorts and definitions; report sampling limits and uncertainty.
Limit: Not all practical qualitative research requires formal statistical power; do not manufacture significance.

## OG-072 · Chapter 15 · Where I come out

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: Research should inform a decision rather than rationalize a conclusion already chosen.
Adaptation: Record the hypothesis and alternative interpretations before interpreting a result.
Limit: The book also admits limitations: long-term campaign value and optimum price remained unresolved for the author.

## OG-074 · Chapter 16 · Sleeping beauties

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: A well-selling but underadvertised product might gain more profit through advertising, but tests may also show no response.
Adaptation: Test incremental profit before assuming the offering needs no communication or guaranteed extra spending.
Limit: Organic success does not establish advertising effectiveness.

## OG-075 · Chapter 16 · Don’t waste time on problem babies

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: The author advises investing in winners rather than endlessly trying to revive failing products.
Adaptation: Compare prospective return, evidence, strategic constraints and opportunity costs.
Limit: Do not automatically abandon a product on one weak result or ignore necessary product fixes.

## OG-076 · Chapter 16 · Don’t dawdle

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: Ogilvy stresses that time matters economically and describes frequent progress review.
Adaptation: Define a bounded review cadence and decision triggers instead of indefinite debate.
Limit: A 30-day interval is a historical example, not a required schedule for every project.

## OG-081 · Chapter 17 · The N.I.H. syndrome

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: The author warns against rejecting ideas merely because they were developed elsewhere and advises local testing and cultural adaptation.
Adaptation: Reuse tested strategic principles while validating local words, laws, conventions and buyers.
Limit: Success abroad does not guarantee success locally; historic national stereotypes are not a basis for profiling.

## OG-082 · Chapter 18 · Claude Hopkins

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: Hopkins emphasized sales and experimentation, though Ogilvy notes blurred boundaries between tested findings and general observations.
Adaptation: Separate empirical tests from the author’s inferences, and keep tests tied to selling outcomes.
Limit: Do not attribute modern rigorous experimentation to an historical anecdote by default.

## OG-089 · Chapter 20 · I predict 13 changes

Locator: chapter and section in the heading.
Basis: author_recommendation
Source summary: The final chapter records a set of historical predictions, including improved research, more information and lower-cost production.
Adaptation: Use these to compare the author’s aspirations with modern evidence only when asked.
Limit: Predictions are not actionable present-day laws or verified current market facts.
