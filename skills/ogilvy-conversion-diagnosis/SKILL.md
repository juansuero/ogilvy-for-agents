## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Observed funnel with denominators and period; Customer feedback and product delivery; Promise, audience and media; Costs, conversion outcomes and retention if known.

1. Map the end-to-end journey: exposure, visit, inquiry, qualification, meeting, purchase, fulfilment, repeat or churn.
2. Compute rates only from compatible, supplied denominators and cohorts; mark missing stages.
3. Separate creative attractiveness, targeting, value proposition, sales process, product quality and experience hypotheses.
4. Audit promised versus delivered benefits, especially overclaims or misunderstood automation capabilities.
5. Prioritize interviews, product fixes, message changes and tests by expected impact, confidence and cost.
6. Propose a staged decision: what to measure now, what to change, and when incremental spend is justified.

Provide the requested result at the appropriate depth. Possible outputs: Funnel and evidence table; Ranked competing hypotheses; Actions, tests and decision gates.
