## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Desired perception; Available touchpoints; Known delivery experience; Audience.

1. Name the intended image as a hypothesis grounded in the brief.
2. Inspect available messages, visual choices, price presentation and service facts.
3. Identify contradictions that affect the promise, not cosmetic differences alone.
4. Preserve consistent strong elements.
5. Propose the smallest changes that strengthen credibility and coherence.
6. Define how to gather perception evidence; do not equate internal intent with public reputation.
