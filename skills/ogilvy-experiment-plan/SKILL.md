## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: Decision and uncertainty; Existing asset/observations; Audience and constraints; Available measurement/traffic.

1. State the decision and competing explanations, separating evidence from guesses.
2. Identify a feasible comparison or learning exercise without pretending all settings support statistical A/B testing.
3. Choose a meaningful outcome and note response-to-sale lag.
4. Keep conditions clear and record what differs; identify confounders.
5. State what outcomes would favor each option or leave uncertainty unresolved.
6. Preserve an effective asset unless evidence warrants change; record results for future review.

### Additional guidance for chapters 12–20

- Distinguish split-run tests of promises, pretests of brand preference and actual commercial outcomes; no recall-as-sale shortcut.
- Keep a suitable incumbent control, compare total economics including retention, and state sample limitations.

Provide the requested result at the appropriate depth. Possible outputs: Hypothesis and bounded test plan; Decision criteria and limitations; Learning record skeleton.
