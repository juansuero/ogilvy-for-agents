## Procedure

Consider the relevant inputs: List of offerings or campaigns; Commercial results and costs; Audience/usage segments; Resources and risks; Known duration and constraints.

1. Separate high-performing offerings, unadvertised successes, uncertain cases and persistent underperformers.
2. Look for profitable sleeping beauties worth a small incremental advertising test.
3. Compare future potential rather than sunk cost; examine whether fixes, repositioning or withdrawal are reasonable.
4. Investigate category heavy users and their distinct motives, without equating category volume with customer profitability.
5. Assess near-term response and brand or customer-value effects; budget realistic learning.
6. Set a review cadence, continuation/stop criteria and reallocations contingent on evidence.
